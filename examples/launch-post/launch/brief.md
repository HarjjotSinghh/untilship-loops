# Launch brief: Tally 1.0

- **What**: Tally is a command-line tool that turns bank CSV exports into a monthly spending summary.
- **Who**: Freelancers and indie founders who do their own books and dread month-end.
- **Problem today**: Exporting from three banks, cleaning the columns by hand and building a pivot table takes about two hours a month.
- **Proof**: Processes 10,000 transactions in 0.4 seconds on a laptop. 312 people used the beta for three months; the median user cut month-end from 2 hours to 9 minutes.
- **Link**: https://tally.example.com
- **Channel**: Show HN and the company blog. Plain, technical, no hype.
